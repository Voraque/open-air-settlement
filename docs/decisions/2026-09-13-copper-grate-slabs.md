# Copper grate slabs — prepared release 1.0.47

Adds More Slabs Stairs & Walls 4.2.0 for Fabric 1.21.1 on both client and server. Includes copper grate slabs in all four oxidation stages and their waxed forms. The upstream mod also adds slab, stair, and wall variants for other building materials.

Verified the upstream SHA-1 and inspected all eight copper grate slab blockstates. Disposable Fabric 0.19.3 / Minecraft 1.21.1 server with Fabric API 0.116.15 loaded recipes without fatal findings, passed ordinary bottom-slab and waxed oxidized top-slab placement assertions, and shut down cleanly. This was an isolated mod test, not a full-pack or rendered-client test.

Source: https://modrinth.com/mod/more-slabs-stairs-and-walls/version/d9CICWIp

Activation requires the same jar on FadeHost and both clients. Do not publish the client update independently of server activation. Two players were online at preparation; activation is pending a suitable restart window.
